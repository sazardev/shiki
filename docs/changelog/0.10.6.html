shiki v0.10.6

Released 2026-11-15.

Share: X Bluesky Reddit Hacker News WhatsApp Telegram Email

Security

  • Dependency and cargo audit fixes. Updated rmcp to 3.10.0 (addresses RUSTSEC-2026-0462), refreshed compatible dependency patches in Cargo.lock (including rustix, lru, notify-rust, zip, and others), and allowlisted transitive glib 0.18.5 (RUSTSEC-2024-0429) in .cargo/audit.toml because the GTK3/tauri desktop stack cannot move to glib >= 0.20 as a patch release and shiki does not use glib::VariantStrIter directly.
